Why Choose This IPA Reader?

This IPA Reader is designed with the goal of providing pronunciation based solely on the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA), without relying on the pronunciation rules of any existing language.

Most IPA readers available today are built upon major languages such as English, Spanish, or German. While this works well for widely spoken languages, it falls short for users who want to learn lesser-known languages or create their own languages (conlangs), as these tools often cannot meet their needs.

To address this gap, I built this website with the assistance of Claude Code. My hope is to provide language learners and language creators with a practical, language-independent tool that helps them achieve their goals more effectively.
